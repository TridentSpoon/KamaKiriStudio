[Flexoki by Steph Ango](https://stephango.com/flexoki) draws inspiration from analog printing inks and warm shades of paper. These generated illustrations follow that direction; they are project additions, not upstream Flexoki or Omarchy artwork.

No gradients across background, modern geometric wallpaper pattern, text, logos, watermark, frame or collage. One finished quiet ink-on-paper wallpaper.
